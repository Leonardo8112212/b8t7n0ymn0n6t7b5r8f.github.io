MINI CRAFT 3D

World: 1000 x 1000 blocks, down to -60 (streamed chunks)

W A S D: walk  |  Shift: sprint  |  Space: jump  |  Mouse: look (or arrow keys)

E: inventory (left click: move stack, right click: split / place one)

T: chat  |  M: menu  |  C: crafting  |  F: smelting

Left click: mine  |  Right click: place block

Keys 1-9 or mouse wheel: choose hotbar slot
